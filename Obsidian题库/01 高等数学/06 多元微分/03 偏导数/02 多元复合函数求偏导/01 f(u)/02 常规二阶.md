## 003. 26版660数一二三第233题

- 题目ID：`2489`
- 题号：`3`
- 题源：26版660数一二三第233题
- 分类：高等数学 / 多元微分 / 偏导数 / 多元复合函数求偏导 / f(u) / 常规二阶
- 选做：否

设 $z = xf(x - y) + yg(x + y)$，其中 f 与 g 有二阶连续导数，则 $\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x^{2}} - \frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial y^{2}}$ 等于

### 选项

A. $2(f'-g')$
B. $f'-g'$
C. $f'+g'$
D. $xf''+yg''$

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> A
>
> **解析**
>
> 由 $z = xf(x - y) + yg(x + y)$ 得 $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}=f+x f^{\prime}+y g^{\prime},\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}=-x f^{\prime}+g+y g^{\prime}$，所以 $\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x^{2}}=f^{\prime}+f^{\prime}+x f^{\prime \prime}+y g^{\prime \prime}=2f^{\prime}+x f^{\prime \prime}+y g^{\prime \prime}$， $\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial y^{2}}=x f^{\prime \prime}+g^{\prime}+g^{\prime}+y g^{\prime \prime}=x f^{\prime \prime}+2g^{\prime}+y g^{\prime \prime}$，故 $\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial x^{2}}-\frac{\partial^{2}z}{\partial y^{2}}=2f^{\prime}-2g^{\prime}=2(f^{\prime}-g^{\prime})$，应选(A)。
