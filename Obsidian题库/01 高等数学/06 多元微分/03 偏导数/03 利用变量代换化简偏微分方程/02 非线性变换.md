## 001. 2019 数二

- 题目ID：`2541`
- 题号：`1`
- 题源：2019 数二
- 分类：高等数学 / 多元微分 / 偏导数 / 利用变量代换化简偏微分方程 / 非线性变换
- 选做：否

已知函数 $u(x,y)$ 满足 $2\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x^{2}}-2\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial y^{2}}+3\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}+3\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=0$，求 $a,b$ 的值使得在变换 $u(x,y)=v(x,y)\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}$ 之下，上述等式可化为函数 $v(x,y)$ 的不含一阶偏导数的等式。

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 由 $u(x,y)=v(x,y)e^{ax+by}$ 可得，$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}\cdot\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}+\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\cdot av=\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\Big(\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}+av\Big)$，$\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\cdot\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}+\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\cdot b v=\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\Big(\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}+b v\Big)$，$\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x^{2}}=a\cdot\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}+av\right)+\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial x^{2}}+a\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}\right)=\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(2a\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}+a^{2}v+\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial x^{2}}\right)$，$\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial y^{2}}=b\cdot\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}+bv\right)+\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial y^{2}}+b\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\right)=\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(2b\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}+b^{2}v+\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial y^{2}}\right)$。代入已知等式可得，$\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left[2\left(2a\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}+a^{2}v+\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial x^{2}}\right)-2\left(2b\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}+b^{2}v+\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial y^{2}}\right)+3\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}+av\right)+3\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}+bv\right)\right]=0$。整理上式，可得 $2\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial x^{2}}-2\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial y^{2}}+\left(4a+3\right)\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}+\left(-4b+3\right)\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}+\left(2a^{2}v-2b^{2}v+3a v+3b v\right)=0$。要使得式中不含有 $v(x,y)$ 的一阶偏导数，需使 $4a+3=0,-4b+3=0$。解得 $a=-\frac{3}{4}$，$b=\frac{3}{4}$。因此，$a=-\frac{3}{4},b=\frac{3}{4}$。

## 002. 900 第四章数二 A 类37

- 题目ID：`2542`
- 题号：`2`
- 题源：900 第四章数二 A 类37
- 分类：高等数学 / 多元微分 / 偏导数 / 利用变量代换化简偏微分方程 / 非线性变换
- 选做：否

已知函数 $u(x,y)$ 满足 $\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x^{2}}-2\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial y^{2}}+3\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}-4\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=0$，求 $a,b$ 的值，使得在变换 $u(x,y)=v(x,y)e^{ax+by}$ 下，上述等式可化为不含 $v(x,y)$ 的一阶偏导数的等式。

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 由 $u(x,y)=v(x,y)\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}$ 可得，
>
> $$
> \frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}\cdot\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}+\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\cdot av=\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}+av\right).
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{\partial u}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\cdot\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}+\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\cdot b v=\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}+b v\right).
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x^{2}}=a\cdot\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}+av\right)+\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial x^{2}}+a\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}\right)=\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(2a\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}+a^{2}v+\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial x^{2}}\right).
> $$
>
> $$
> \frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial y^{2}}=b\cdot\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}+bv\right)+\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial y^{2}}+b\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\right)=\mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left(2b\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}+b^{2}v+\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial y^{2}}\right).
> $$
>
> 代入已知等式可得，
>
> $$
> \mathrm{e}^{ax+by}\left[\left(2a\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}+a^{2}v+\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial x^{2}}\right)-2\left(2b\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}+b^{2}v+\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial y^{2}}\right)+3\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}+av\right)-4\left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}+bv\right)\right]=0.
> $$
>
> 整理上式，可得
>
> $$
> \frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial x^{2}}-2\frac{\partial^{2}v}{\partial y^{2}}+(2a+3)\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}-(4b+4)\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}+(a^{2}v-2b^{2}v+3a v-4b v)=0.
> $$
>
> 要使得式中不含有 $v(x,y)$ 的一阶偏导数，需使 $2a+3=0,4b+4=0$。解得 $a=-\frac{3}{2},b=-1$。
> 因此，$a=-\frac{3}{2},b=-1$。

^q-2542
