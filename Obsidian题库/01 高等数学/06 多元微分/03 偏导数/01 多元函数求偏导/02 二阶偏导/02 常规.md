> **解析**
>
> 第一步：求一阶偏导。$f(x,\ y)=x^{2}\arctan\frac{y}{x}-y^{2}\arctan\frac{x}{y}$。对 x 求导时，将 y 看成常数。$\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}=2x\arctan\frac{y}{x}+x^{2}\cdot\frac{1}{1+\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)^{2}}\cdot\left(-\frac{y}{x^{2}}\right)-y^{2}\cdot\frac{1}{1+\left(\frac{x}{y}\right)^{2}}\cdot\frac{1}{y}=2x\arctan\frac{y}{x}-\frac{x^{2}y}{x^{2}+y^{2}}-\frac{y^{3}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}$。第二步：求二阶偏导。$\frac{\partial^{2}f}{\partial x\partial y}=\frac{\partial\left(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\right)}{\partial y}$。对 y 求导时，将 x 看成常数。$\begin{aligned}&=\frac{\partial\left(2x\arctan\frac{y}{x}-\frac{x^{2}y}{x^{2}+y^{2}}-\frac{y^{3}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}\right)}{\partial y}\\&=2x\cdot\frac{1}{1+\left(\frac{y}{x}\right)^{2}}\cdot\frac{1}{x}-\frac{x^{2}\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)-x^{2}y\cdot2y}{\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)^{2}}-\frac{3y^{2}\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)-y^{3}\cdot2y}{(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}}\\&=\frac{2x^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}-\frac{x^{4}-x^{2}y^{2}}{(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}}-\frac{3x^{2}y^{2}+y^{4}}{(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}}\\&=\frac{2x^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}-\frac{x^{4}-x^{2}y^{2}+3x^{2}y^{2}+y^{4}}{(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}}\\&=\frac{2x^{2}\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)}{(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}}-\frac{x^{4}+2x^{2}y^{2}+y^{4}}{(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}}\\&=\frac{2x^{4}+2x^{2}y^{2}-x^{4}-2x^{2}y^{2}-y^{4}}{(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}}\\&=\frac{x^{4}-y^{4}}{(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}}=\frac{\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)\left(x^{2}-y^{2}\right)}{\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)^{2}}\\&=\frac{x^{2}-y^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}.\end{aligned}$

设 $u = e^{-x} \sin \frac{x}{y}$，则 $\frac{\partial^{2} u}{\partial x \partial y}$ 在点 $\left(2, \frac{1}{\pi}\right)$ 处的值为 ___

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 第一步：求 $\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}$。由于 $u = e^{-x} \sin \frac{x}{y}$，则对 x 求偏导，y 看做常数。$\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}=-e^{-x}\sin\frac{x}{y}+e^{-x}\cos\frac{x}{y}\cdot\frac{1}{y}=e^{-x}\left(\frac{1}{y}\cos\frac{x}{y}-\sin\frac{x}{y}\right)$。第二步：求 $\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x\partial y}$。故 $\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x\partial y}=\frac{\partial}{\partial y}\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}\right)$。对 y 求偏导，x 看做常数。$\begin{aligned}&=e^{-x}\left[-\frac{1}{y^{2}}\cos\frac{x}{y}+\frac{1}{y}\cdot\left(-\sin\frac{x}{y}\right)\left(-\frac{x}{y^{2}}\right)-\cos\frac{x}{y}\cdot\left(-\frac{x}{y^{2}}\right)\right]\\&=e^{-x}\left(\frac{x}{y^{3}}\sin\frac{x}{y}+\frac{x-1}{y^{2}}\cos\frac{x}{y}\right)\end{aligned}$。于是 $\frac{\partial^{2}u}{\partial x\partial y}\Big\vert_{\left(2,\ \frac{1}{\pi}\right)}=\mathrm{e}^{-2}\left(2\pi^{3}\sin2\pi+\pi^{2}\cos2\pi\right)=\mathrm{e}^{-2}\cdot\pi^{2}=\left(\frac{\pi}{\mathrm{e}}\right)^{2}$。
