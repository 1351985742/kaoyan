## 003. 2021 数二

曲线 $(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}=x^{2}-y^{2}(x\geqslant0,y\geqslant0)$ 与 x 轴围成的区域为 D，求 $\iint_{D}xy\,dx\,dy$.

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 分析：本题主要考查二重积分的计算。曲线 $(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}=x^{2}-y^{2}(x\geqslant0,y\geqslant0)$ 为双纽线在第一象限的部分。由该曲线与 x 轴所围区域 D 在直角坐标下的表示较难写，但是在极坐标下的表示较简单，故可以考虑在极坐标下计算所求二重积分。解：曲线 $(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}=x^{2}-y^{2}(x\geqslant0,y\geqslant0)$ 与 x 轴围成的区域 D 如图所示。写出曲线 $(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}=x^{2}-y^{2}$ 的极坐标方程。将 $x=r\cos\theta, y=r\sin\theta$ 代入 $(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}=x^{2}-y^{2}$ 可得， $r^{4}=r^{2}\cos2\theta$，即 $r^{2}=\cos2\theta$。于是，区域 D 的极坐标表示为 
>
> $$
> D=\left\{(r,\theta)\middle|0\leqslant r\leqslant\sqrt{\cos2\theta},0\leqslant\theta\leqslant\frac{\pi}{4}\right\}.
> $$
>
>  因此， 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}\iint\limits_{D}xy\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y&\xlongequal{ 极坐标 }\iint\limits_{D}r\cos\theta\cdot r\sin\theta\cdot r\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}\theta=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\sin\theta\cos\theta\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{\sqrt{\cos2\theta}}r^{3}\mathrm{d}r\\&=\left.\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\sin2\theta\cdot\frac{r^{4}}{4}\right|_{0}^{\sqrt{\cos2\theta}}\mathrm{d}\theta=\left.\frac{1}{8}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\sin2\theta\cdot\cos^{2}2\theta\mathrm{d}\theta\right.\\&=-\left.\frac{1}{16}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\cos^{2}2\theta\mathrm{d}(\cos2\theta)\right.\\&=-\left.\frac{1}{16}\times\left(0-\frac{1}{3}\right)=\frac{1}{48}\right..\end{aligned}
> $$

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 解 在极坐标系下计算。由 $\left\{\begin{aligned}x&=r\cos\theta,\\ y&=r\sin\theta\end{aligned}\right.$，可知，曲线 $x^{2}+y^{2}-xy=1$, $x^{2}+y^{2}-xy=2$ 的极坐标方程分别为 $r^{2}-r^{2}\sin\theta\cos\theta=1$, $r^{2}-r^{2}\sin\theta\cos\theta=2$，即 $r=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\sin\theta\cos\theta}}$, $r=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{1-\sin\theta\cos\theta}}$。直线 $y=\sqrt{3}x$ 的极坐标方程为 $\theta=\frac{\pi}{3}$。于是，区域 D 在极坐标系下的表示为 $D=\left\{(r,\theta)\left|\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\sin\theta\cos\theta}}\leqslant r\leqslant\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{1-\sin\theta\cos\theta}},0\leqslant\theta\leqslant\frac{\pi}{3}\right.\right\}$。因此，$\begin{aligned}\iint_{D}\frac{1}{3x^{2}+y^{2}}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y&\xlongequal{ 极坐标 }\iint_{D}\frac{rdr\mathrm{d}\theta}{3r^{2}\cos^{2}\theta+r^{2}\sin^{2}\theta}=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\sin\theta\cos\theta}}}^{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{1-\sin\theta\cos\theta}}}\frac{rdr}{3r^{2}\cos^{2}\theta+r^{2}\sin^{2}\theta}\\&=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}\frac{1}{3\cos^{2}\theta+\sin^{2}\theta}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\sin\theta\cos\theta}}}^{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{1-\sin\theta\cos\theta}}}\frac{\mathrm{d}(r^{2})}{r^{2}}\\&=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}\frac{1}{3\cos^{2}\theta+\sin^{2}\theta}\cdot\ln r^{2}\Bigg|_{\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-\sin\theta\cos\theta}}}^{\frac{\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{1-\sin\theta\cos\theta}}}\mathrm{d}\theta=\frac{\ln2}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}\frac{\mathrm{d}\theta}{\cos^{2}\theta(3+\tan^{2}\theta)}\\&=\ln2\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{3}}\frac{\mathrm{d}(\tan\theta)}{3+\tan^{2}\theta}\xlongequal{t=\tan\theta}\frac{\ln2}{2}\int_{0}^{\sqrt{3}}\frac{\mathrm{d}t}{3+t^{2}}=\frac{\ln2}{2\sqrt{3}}\int_{0}^{\sqrt{3}}\frac{\mathrm{d}\left(\frac{t}{\sqrt{3}}\right)}{1+\left(\frac{t}{\sqrt{3}}\right)^{2}}\\&=\frac{\sqrt{3}\ln2}{6}\arctan\frac{t}{\sqrt{3}}\Bigg|_{0}^{\sqrt{3}}=\frac{\sqrt{3}\ln2}{6}\cdot\frac{\pi}{4}=\frac{\sqrt{3}\pi\ln2}{24}.\end{aligned}$

## 007. 24 李林六数二第二套

- 题目ID：`2875`
- 题号：`7`
- 题源：24 李林六数二第二套
- 分类：高等数学 / 二重积分 / 计算 / 极坐标 / 积分区域难画
- 选做：否

设 $f(x,y)=\left\{\begin{aligned}&x-y+1,&x+y\leqslant1\\ &\frac{(x+y)^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}},&x+y>1\end{aligned}\right.$， $D=\left\{(x,y)\mid x+y+xy\geqslant1,x^{2}+y^{2}\leqslant1, x \geqslant 0, y \geqslant 0 \right\}$，计算 $I = \iint_{D} f(x, y) \, dx \, dy.$

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 由 $x + y + xy = 1$，得 $y = \frac{1 - x}{1 + x}$，即 $y + 1 = \frac{2}{1 + x}$，它是以 x = -1，y = -1 为渐近线的双曲线。如图所示， $x + y = 1$ 将 D 分成 $D_{1}$ 与 $D_{2}$。
>
> $$
> I=\iint_{D_{1}}(x-y+1)dx dy+\iint_{D_{2}}\frac{(x+y)^{2}}{x^{2}+y^{2}}dx dy
> $$
>
>  
> 由于 $D_{1}$ 关于直线 y = x 对称，所以
>
> $$
> \iint_{D_{1}}(x-y)\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=\frac{1}{2}\iint_{D_{1}}(x-y+y-x)\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=0,
> $$
>
>  
>
> $$
> I_{1}=0+\iint_{D_{1}}^{}dx\ dy=\int_{0}^{1}dx\int_{\frac{1-x}{1+x}}^{1-x}dy=\int_{0}^{1}\left(1-x-\frac{1-x}{1+x}\right)dx=\frac{3}{2}-2\ln2,
> $$
>
>  
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}I_{2}=&\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{\frac{1}{\cos\theta+\sin\theta}}^{1}\frac{r^{2}(\cos\theta+\sin\theta)^{2}}{r^{2}}\cdot r\mathrm{d}r\\=&\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}(\cos\theta+\sin\theta)^{2}\cdot\frac{1}{2}r^{2}\left|_{\frac{1}{\cos\theta+\sin\theta}}^{1}\mathrm{d}\theta\\=&\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}(\cos\theta+\sin\theta)^{2}\left(1-\frac{1}{(\cos\theta+\sin\theta)^{2}}\right)\mathrm{d}\theta\\=&\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}(1+\sin2\theta)\mathrm{d}\theta-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\mathrm{d}\theta\\=&\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin2\theta\mathrm{d}\theta=\frac{1}{4}(-\cos2\theta)\left|_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\right|_{0}=\frac{1}{2}.\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  
> 故 $I=I_{1}+I_{2}=\frac{3}{2}-2\ln2+\frac{1}{2}=2-2\ln2.$

## 008. 25 版 ☀️ 0.1w 基础 15; 24 版 ☀️ 8 套数二第六套

设平面区域 $D = \{(x, y) \mid 2x^{2} + y^{2} \leqslant 2\sqrt{x^{2} + y^{2}}, y \geqslant x \geqslant 0\}$，若 $\iint_{D} \frac{f(x, y)}{\sqrt{1 - x^{2}}} \, \mathrm{d}\sigma = a > 0$， $f(x,y)$ 是 D 上的连续函数。
(1) 计算 $\iint_{D} \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}} d\sigma;$
(2) 证明: 存在 $(\xi, \eta) \in D$，使得 $|f(\xi, \eta)| \geqslant \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\pi} a.$

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> (1) $2x^{2}+y^{2}=2\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}$ 在极坐标系下为 $r(1+\cos^{2}\theta)=2$，直角坐标系下的积分区域D如图所示。则
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}\iint_{D}\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}\mathrm{d}\sigma&=\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{\frac{2}{1+\cos^{2}\theta}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-r^{2}\cos^{2}\theta}}r\mathrm{d}r=2\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\frac{1}{1+\cos^{2}\theta}\mathrm{d}\theta\\ &=2\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\frac{\sec^{2}\theta}{\sec^{2}\theta+1}\mathrm{d}\theta=2\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\frac{\mathrm{d}(\tan\theta)}{2+\tan^{2}\theta}\\ &\xlongequal{u=\tan\theta}2\int_{1}^{+\infty}\frac{\mathrm{d}u}{2+u^{2}}=\sqrt{2}\arctan\frac{u}{\sqrt{2}}\bigg|_{1}^{+\infty}=\sqrt{2}\cdot\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\arctan\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)\\ &=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\pi-\sqrt{2}\arctan\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}.\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  
> (2) 由积分保号性可得，
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}&a=\iint_{D}\frac{f(x,y)}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}\mathrm{d}\sigma=\left|\iint_{D}\frac{f(x,y)}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}\mathrm{d}\sigma\right|\leqslant\iint_{D}\frac{\left|f(x,y)\right|}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}\mathrm{d}\sigma\\ &\xlongequal{ 积分中值 }\left|f(\xi,\eta)\right|\left|\iint_{D}\frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}\mathrm{d}\sigma\right.\\ &=\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\pi-\sqrt{2}\arctan\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)\left|f(\xi,\eta)\right|((\xi,\eta)\in D),\end{aligned}

## 009. 24 李艳芳三套数二第三套

> **解析**
>

>
> $$

>
> $$
> \int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin^{n}x\mathrm{d}x\xlongequal{t=\frac{\pi}{2}-x}-\int_{\frac{\pi}{2}}^{0}\sin^{n}\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-t\right)\mathrm{d}t=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\cos^{n}x\mathrm{d}x.

>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}\iint_{D_{1}}\frac{\sqrt{x^{6}+y^{6}}}{\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)^{2}}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y&=4\iint_{D}\frac{\sqrt{x^{6}+y^{6}}}{\left(x^{2}+y^{2}\right)^{2}}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=\frac{5\pi}{8}.\end{aligned}
> $$

> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 将区域边界曲线转化为极坐标，得 $(x^{2}+y^{2})^{3}=4x^{2}y^{2}: r=\sin2\theta$， $(x^{2}+y^{2})^{3}=16x^{2}y^{2}: r=2\sin2\theta$。 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}I&=\iint_{D}(x^{2}+y^{2}+xy)\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{\sin2\theta}^{2\sin2\theta}(r^{2}+r^{2}\cos\theta\sin\theta)r\mathrm{d}r\\&=\frac{15}{4}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}(\sin^{4}2\theta+\sin^{4}2\theta\cos\theta\sin\theta)\mathrm{d}\theta.\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  
>
> $$
> \frac{15}{4}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin^{4}2\theta\mathrm{d}\theta=\frac{15}{8}\int_{0}^{\pi}\sin^{4}t\mathrm{d}t=\frac{15}{4}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin^{4}t\mathrm{d}t=\frac{15}{4}\cdot\frac{3}{4}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2}=\frac{45}{64}\pi,
> $$
>
>  
>
> $$
> \frac{15}{4}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin^{4}2\theta\cos\theta\sin\theta\mathrm{d}\theta=\frac{15}{16}\int_{0}^{\pi}\sin^{5}t\mathrm{d}t=\frac{15}{8}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin^{5}t\mathrm{d}t=\frac{15}{8}\cdot\frac{4}{5}\cdot\frac{2}{3}=1,
> $$
>
>  故 $I=\frac{45}{64}\pi+1.$

## 011. 姜晓千真题同源 150

- 题目ID：`2879`
- 题号：`11`
- 题源：姜晓千真题同源 150
- 分类：高等数学 / 二重积分 / 计算 / 极坐标 / 积分区域难画
- 选做：否

例 6.5 (1) 设区域 $D$ 由曲线 $(x^{2} + y^{2})^{3} = 4x^{2}y^{2}$ 围成，计算积分 $\iint_{D} |xy| \, dx \, dy$; (2) 求曲线 $(x^{2} + y^{2})^{2} = 2a^{2}(x^{2} - y^{2})(a > 0)$ 与 $x^{2} + y^{2} \geqslant a^{2}$ 所围区域的面积 $A$。

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 【详解】（1）设 $x = r \cos \theta, y = r \sin \theta$，代入 $(x^{2} + y^{2})^{3} = 4x^{2}y^{2}$ 得 $r^{2} = \sin^{2}2\theta$。 $D_{1}:0\leqslant\theta\leqslant\frac{\pi}{2},0\leqslant r\leqslant\sin2\theta$， 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}\iint_{D}\mid x y\mid\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y&=4\iint_{D_{1}}x y\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=4\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{\sin2\theta}r^{3}\cos\theta\sin\theta\mathrm{d}r\\&=\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\cos\theta\sin\theta\sin^{4}2\theta\mathrm{d}\theta=\frac{1}{4}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin^{5}2\theta\mathrm{d}2\theta\\&=\frac{1}{4}\int_{0}^{\pi}\sin^{5}t\mathrm{d}t=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin^{5}t\mathrm{d}t=\frac{1}{2}\times\frac{4}{5}\times\frac{2}{3}=\frac{4}{15}.\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  （2）由 $(x^{2}+y^{2})^{2}=2a^{2}(x^{2}-y^{2})$，得 $r^{2}=2a^{2}\cos2\theta$。设 $D_{1}:0 \leqslant \theta \leqslant \frac{\pi}{6}$， $a \leqslant r \leqslant a\sqrt{2\cos 2\theta}$，则 $\cos 2\theta = \frac{1}{2}$， $2\theta = \frac{\pi}{3}$。 
>
> $$
> A=4\iint_{D_{1}}dxdy=4\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{6}}d\theta\int_{a}^{a\sqrt{2\cos2\theta}}rdr=a^{2}\left(\sqrt{3}-\frac{\pi}{3}\right).
> $$
