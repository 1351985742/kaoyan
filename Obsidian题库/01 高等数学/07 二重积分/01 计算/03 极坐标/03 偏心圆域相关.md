## 006. 姜晓千真题同源 150

- 题目ID：`2844`
- 题号：`6`
- 题源：姜晓千真题同源 150
- 分类：高等数学 / 二重积分 / 计算 / 极坐标 / 偏心圆域相关
- 选做：否

设区域 $D=\left\{(x,y)\mid x^{2}+y^{2}\leqslant2x+2y\right\}$，计算积分 $\iint_{D}(x+y^{2})dxdy.$

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 由题设知 $D=\left\{(x,y)\mid(x-1)^{2}+(y-1)^{2}\leqslant2\right\}$。由形心公式得 $\iint_{D}x\,dx\,dy=\overline{x}S_{D}=2\pi.$ 方法1： 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}\iint_{D}y^{2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y&=\frac{1}{2}\iint_{D}(x^{2}+y^{2})\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=\frac{1}{2}\int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{2\left(\sin\theta+\cos\theta\right)}r^{2}r\mathrm{d}r=2\int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\left(\sin\theta+\cos\theta\right)^{1}\mathrm{d}\theta\\&=8\int_{-\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\sin^{4}\left(\theta+\frac{\pi}{4}\right)\mathrm{d}\theta\frac{\theta+\frac{\pi}{4}=t}{}8\int_{0}^{\pi}\sin^{4}t\mathrm{d}t=16\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\sin^{4}t\mathrm{d}t\\&=16\cdot\frac{3}{4}\cdot\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2}=3\pi.\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  方法2：令 $x-1=r\cos\theta, y-1=r\sin\theta$，则 $\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=r\mathrm{d}r\mathrm{d}\theta$，得 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}\iint_{D}y^{2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y&=\int_{0}^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{\sqrt{2}}(1+r\sin\theta)^{2}r\mathrm{d}r=\int_{0}^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{\sqrt{2}}(r+2r^{2}\sin\theta+r^{3}\sin^{2}\theta)\mathrm{d}r\\&=\int_{0}^{2\pi}\left(1+\frac{4\sqrt{2}}{3}\sin\theta+\sin^{2}\theta\right)\mathrm{d}\theta=2\pi+4\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{2}}\sin^{2}\theta\mathrm{d}\theta\\&=2\pi+4\cdot\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{\pi}{2}=3\pi.\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  方法3：令 $x-1=u, y-1=v$，则 $\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=\mathrm{d}u\mathrm{d}v,D^{\prime}=\left\{(u,v)\mid u^{2}+v^{2}\leqslant2\right\}$，得 
>
> $$
> \begin{align*}\iint_{D}y^{2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y&=\iint_{D}(v+1)^{2}\mathrm{d}u\mathrm{d}v=\iint_{D}(v^{2}+2v+1)\mathrm{d}u\mathrm{d}v=\iint_{D}v^{2}\mathrm{d}u\mathrm{d}v+2\pi\\&=\frac{1}{2}\iint_{D}(u^{2}+v^{2})\mathrm{d}u\mathrm{d}v+2\pi=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{2\pi}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{\sqrt{2}}r^{2}r\mathrm{d}r+2\pi=\pi+2\pi=3\pi.\end{align*}
> $$
>
>  综上， $\iint\limits_{D}(x+y^{2})\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=2\pi+3\pi=5\pi.$
