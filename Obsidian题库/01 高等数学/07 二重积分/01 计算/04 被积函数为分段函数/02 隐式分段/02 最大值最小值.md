## 001. 1995 数三

- 题目ID：`2920`
- 题号：`1`
- 题源：1995 数三
- 分类：高等数学 / 二重积分 / 计算 / 被积函数为分段函数 / 隐式分段 / 最大值最小值
- 选做：否

计算二次积分 $I=\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\min\{x,y\}e^{-(x^{2}+y^{2})}dx dy$

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 方法一：令 $D=\left\{(x,y)\mid x^{2}+y^{2}\leqslant R^{2}\right\}$，则 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}&\iint_{D}\min\{x,y\}\mathrm{e}^{-(x^{2}+y^{2})}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y\\=&\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\sin\theta\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{R}r^{2}\mathrm{e}^{-r^{2}}\mathrm{d}r+\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{5\pi}{4}}\cos\theta\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{R}r^{2}\mathrm{e}^{-r^{2}}\mathrm{d}r+\int_{\frac{5\pi}{4}}^{\frac{2\pi}{4}}\sin\theta\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{R}r^{2}\mathrm{e}^{-r^{2}}\mathrm{d}r\\=&\left(1-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)\int_{0}^{R}r^{2}\mathrm{e}^{-r^{2}}\mathrm{d}r-\sqrt{2}\int_{0}^{R}r^{2}\mathrm{e}^{-r^{2}}\mathrm{d}r-\left(1+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)\int_{0}^{R}r^{2}\mathrm{e}^{-r^{2}}\mathrm{d}r\\=&-2\sqrt{2}\int_{0}^{R}r^{2}\mathrm{e}^{-r^{2}}\mathrm{d}r,\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  故 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned} \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\min\{x,y\}\mathrm{e}^{-(x^{2}+y^{2})}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y&=\lim_{R\to+\infty}\iint_{D}\min\{x,y\}\mathrm{e}^{-(x^{2}+y^{2})}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y\\ &=-2\sqrt{2}\int_{0}^{+\infty}r^{2}\mathrm{e}^{-r^{2}}\mathrm{d}r\xlongequal{r^{2}=t}-\sqrt{2}\int_{0}^{+\infty}t^{\frac{1}{2}}\mathrm{e}^{-t}\mathrm{d}t\\ &=-\sqrt{2}\Gamma\Big(\frac{1}{2}+1\Big)=-\frac{\sqrt{2\pi}}{2}.\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  方法二：令 $D_{1}=\left\{(x,y)\mid y<x\right\}$，由对称性得 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty}\min(x,y)\mathrm{e}^{-(x^{2}+y^{2})}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y&=2\iint_{D_{1}}y\mathrm{e}^{-(x^{2}+y^{2})}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y=2\int_{-\frac{3}{4}\pi}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{+\infty}r^{2}\sin\theta\cdot\mathrm{e}^{-r^{2}}\mathrm{d}r\\&=2\int_{-\frac{3}{4}\pi}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\sin\theta\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{0}^{+\infty}r^{2}\mathrm{e}^{-r^{2}}\mathrm{d}r=-2\sqrt{2}\int_{0}^{+\infty}r^{2}\mathrm{e}^{-r^{2}}\mathrm{d}r\\&\xlongequal{r^{2}=t}-\sqrt{2}\int_{0}^{+\infty}t^{\frac{1}{2}}\mathrm{e}^{-t}\mathrm{d}t=-\sqrt{2}\Gamma\Big(\frac{1}{2}+1\Big)=-\frac{\sqrt{2\pi}}{2}.\end{aligned}
> $$

## 002. 2002 数一

计算二重积分 $\iint_{D}e^{\max\left\{x^{2},y^{2}\right\}}dxdy$，其中 $D=\left\{(x,y)|0\leqslant x\leqslant1,0\leqslant y\leqslant1\right\}$.

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 令 $D_{1}=\{(x,y) \in D \mid x \ge y\}$，$D_{2}=\{(x,y) \in D \mid x \le y\}$，则 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}I&=\iint_{D_{1}}e^{x^{2}}dx dy+\iint_{D_{2}}e^{y^{2}}dx dy\\&=\int_{0}^{1}e^{x^{2}}dx\int_{0}^{x}dy+\int_{0}^{1}e^{y^{2}}dy\int_{0}^{y}dx\\&=\int_{0}^{1}xe^{x^{2}}dx+\int_{0}^{1}ye^{y^{2}}dy\\&=2\cdot\frac{1}{2}(e-1)\\&=e-1\end{aligned}
> $$

## 004. 复习全书（红）

计算二重积分 $\iint_{D}\sin x\sin y\max\left\{x,y\right\}dxdy$，其中 $D=\left\{(x,y)\mid0\leq x\leq\pi,0\leq y\leq\pi\right\}$

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 令 $D_{1}=\{(x,y) \in D \mid x \ge y\}$，$D_{2}=\{(x,y) \in D \mid x \le y\}$，由对称性得 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}I&=\iint_{D_{1}}x\sin x\sin y\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y+\iint_{D_{2}}y\sin x\sin y\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y\\&=\int_{0}^{\pi}x\sin x\mathrm{d}x\int_{0}^{x}\sin y\mathrm{d}y+\int_{0}^{\pi}y\sin y\mathrm{d}y\int_{0}^{y}\sin x\mathrm{d}x\\&=\int_{0}^{\pi}x\sin x\left(1-\cos x\right)\mathrm{d}x+\int_{0}^{\pi}y\sin y\left(1-\cos y\right)\mathrm{d}y\\&=2\int_{0}^{\pi}x\sin x\left(1-\cos x\right)\mathrm{d}x\\&=-2\int_{0}^{\pi}x\mathrm{d}\cos x+\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\pi}x\mathrm{d}\cos2x\\&=-2\left(x\cos x\right)\Big|_{0}^{\pi}+\int_{0}^{\pi}\cos x\mathrm{d}x+\frac{1}{2}\left(x\cos2x\right)\Big|_{0}^{\pi}-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\pi}\cos2x\mathrm{d}x\\&=2\pi+\frac{\pi}{2}=\frac{5}{2}\pi\end{aligned}
> $$

## 005. 880 基础解答 7

- 题目ID：`2924`
- 题号：`5`
- 题源：880 基础解答 7
- 分类：高等数学 / 二重积分 / 计算 / 被积函数为分段函数 / 隐式分段 / 最大值最小值
- 选做：否

设 $D=\left\{(x,y)\mid0\leqslant x\leqslant1,0\leqslant y\leqslant1\right\}$，计算 $I=\iint_{D}\max\left\{2x-x^{2},(1-y)^{2}\right\}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y$

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 当 $2x - x^{2} = (1 - y)^{2}$ 时，有 $(x - 1)^{2} + (y - 1)^{2} = 1$。圆周 $(x-1)^{2}+(y-1)^{2}=1$ 将 D 分为 $D_{1}$ 与 $D_{2}$ 两个区域，其中 $D_{1}$ 为圆内部分，$D_{2}$ 为圆外部分。于是 $\max\left\{2x-x^{2},\left(1-y\right)^{2}\right\}=\left\{\begin{aligned}&(1-y)^{2},&(x,y)\in D_{1},\\ &2x-x^{2},&(x,y)\in D_{2}.\end{aligned}\right.$ 则 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}I&=\iint_{D_{1}}^{}(1-y)^{2}\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y+\iint_{D_{2}}^{}(2x-x^{2})\mathrm{d}x\mathrm{d}y\\ &=\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}y\int_{0}^{1-\sqrt{2y-y^{2}}}(1-y)^{2}\mathrm{d}x+\int_{0}^{1}\mathrm{d}x\int_{1-\sqrt{2x-x^{2}}}^{1}(2x-x^{2})\mathrm{d}y\\ &=\int_{0}^{1}(1-y)^{2}(1-\sqrt{2y-y^{2}})\mathrm{d}y+\int_{0}^{1}(2x-x^{2})[1-(1-\sqrt{2x-x^{2}})]\mathrm{d}x\\ &=\int_{0}^{1}(1-y)^{2}\mathrm{d}y-\int_{0}^{1}(1-y)^{2}\sqrt{1-(y-1)^{2}}\mathrm{d}y+\int_{0}^{1}(2x-x^{2})\sqrt{2x-x^{2}}\mathrm{d}x\\ &=\frac{1}{3}+\int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{1-(x-1)^{2}}\mathrm{d}(x-1)-2\int_{0}^{1}(x-1)^{2}\sqrt{1-(x-1)^{2}}\mathrm{d}(x-1)\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  计算得 $\int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{1-(x-1)^{2}}\mathrm{d}(x-1)=\frac{\pi}{4}$，$\int_{0}^{1}(x-1)^{2}\sqrt{1-(x-1)^{2}}\mathrm{d}(x-1)=\frac{\pi}{4}-\frac{3\pi}{16}$，代入得最终结果。

## 008. 25 版 0.1w 强化 37

- 题目ID：`2927`
- 题号：`8`
- 题源：25 版 0.1w 强化 37
- 分类：高等数学 / 二重积分 / 计算 / 被积函数为分段函数 / 隐式分段 / 最大值最小值
- 选做：否

设 $f(x,y)=\max\left\{\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}},1\right\},D=\left\{(x,y)\parallel x\mid\leqslant y\leqslant1\right\}.$ 求 $\iint\limits_{D}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}\sigma.$

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 如图所示，将D分成三块，中间一块记为 $D_{3}$，左、右两块分别记为 $D_{1}$ 与 $D_{2}$，则
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}f(x,y)&=\max\{\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}},1\}\\&=\begin{cases}\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}},&(x,y)\in D_{1}\bigcup D_{2},\\1,&(x,y)\in D_{3}.\end{cases}\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}\iint_{D}f(x,y)\mathrm{d}\sigma&=\iint_{D_{1}\cup D_{2}}\sqrt{x^{2}+y^{2}}\mathrm{d}\sigma+\iint_{D_{3}}\mathrm{d}\sigma\\&=\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}}\mathrm{d}\theta\int_{1}^{\frac{1}{\sin\theta}}r^{2}\mathrm{d}r+\frac{\pi}{4}\\&=\frac{1}{3}\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}}(\csc^{3}\theta-1)\mathrm{d}\theta+\frac{\pi}{4}\\&=\frac{1}{3}\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}}\csc^{3}\theta\mathrm{d}\theta+\frac{\pi}{12}.\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  
> 而
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}}\csc^{3}\theta\mathrm{d}\theta=&\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}}\csc\theta\csc^{2}\theta\mathrm{d}\theta\\=&-\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}}\csc\theta\mathrm{d}(\cot\theta)=-\left.\left(\csc\theta\cot\theta\right|_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}}+\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}}\cot^{2}\theta\csc\theta\mathrm{d}\theta\right)\\=&-\left[-\sqrt{2}-\sqrt{2}+\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}}(\csc^{2}\theta-1)\csc\theta\mathrm{d}\theta\right],\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  
> 所以
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}}\csc^{3}\theta\mathrm{d}\theta&=\sqrt{2}+\frac{1}{2}\int_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}}\csc\theta\mathrm{d}\theta\\&=\sqrt{2}+\frac{1}{2}(\ln|\csc\theta-\cot\theta|)\bigg|_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{3\pi}{4}}\\&=\sqrt{2}+\frac{1}{2}\left[\ln(\sqrt{2}+1)-\ln(\sqrt{2}-1)\right]\\&=\sqrt{2}+\ln(\sqrt{2}+1).\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  
> 故原式 $=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{3}+\frac{1}{3}\ln(\sqrt{2}+1)+\frac{\pi}{12}.$
