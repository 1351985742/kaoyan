## 008. 900 第三章数二 B 类 44

- 题目ID：`2045`

- 选做：否

求 $\int_{0}^{1}\frac{x}{1+x^{2}}f(x)dx$，其中 $f(x)=\int_{1}^{x}\frac{\arctan t}{(1+t^{2})^{\frac{3}{2}}\ln(1+t^{2})}\mathrm{d}t$。

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> 无
>
> **解析**
>
> 由 $f(x)$ 的表达式可得， $f(1)=0$。进一步由变限积分的求导公式可得， 
>
> $$
> f^{\prime}(x)=\frac{\arctan x}{\left(1+x^{2}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}\ln\left(1+x^{2}\right)}.
> $$
>
>  因此， 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{x}{1+x^{2}}f(x)\mathrm{d}x&=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}f(x)\mathrm{d}\left[\ln\left(1+x^{2}\right)\right]=\frac{1}{2}\left[f(x)\ln\left(1+x^{2}\right)\right.\left.\right|_{0}^{1}-\int_{0}^{1}\ln(1+x^{2})f^{\prime}(x)\mathrm{d}x\Big]\\&=-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\ln(1+x^{2})\cdot\frac{\arctan x}{\left(1+x^{2}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}\ln(1+x^{2})}\mathrm{d}x=-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}\frac{\arctan x}{\left(1+x^{2}\right)^{\frac{3}{2}}}\mathrm{d}x\\&\xlongequal{x=\tan t}-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\frac{t}{\sec^{3}t}\cdot\sec^{2}t\mathrm{d}t=-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}t\cos t\mathrm{d}t=-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}t\mathrm{d}(\sin t)\\&=-\frac{1}{2}\left(t\sin t\right|_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}-\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\sin t\mathrm{d}t\right)=-\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{4}\cdot\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}+\cos t\right|_{\frac{\pi}{4}}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\Big)\\&=-\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}\pi}{8}+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}-1\right)=-\frac{1}{16}(\sqrt{2}\pi+4\sqrt{2}-8).\end{aligned}
> $$

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> $\frac{1}{9}(1-2\sqrt{2})$
>
> **解析**
>
> 方法一 由 $f(x)=\int_{1}^{x}\sqrt{1+t^{3}}\,\mathrm{d}t$，可得 $f'(x)=\sqrt{1+x^{3}}$，且 $f(1)=0$，从而 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}\int_{0}^{1}xf(x)\mathrm{d}x&=\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}f(x)\mathrm{d}(x^{2})=\frac{1}{2}\Big[x^{2}f(x)\Big|_{0}^{1}-\int_{0}^{1}x^{2}f^{\prime}(x)\mathrm{d}x\Big]\\&=-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}x^{2}\sqrt{1+x^{3}}\mathrm{d}x=-\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{2}{9}(1+x^{3})^{\frac{3}{2}}\Big|_{0}^{1}\\&=\frac{1}{9}(1-2\sqrt{2}).\end{aligned}
> $$
>
>  方法二(此方法学完二重积分再看) 将 $f(x)=\int_{1}^{x}\sqrt{1+t^{3}}dt$ 代入所求表达式，有 
>
> $$
> \int_{0}^{1}x f(x)\mathrm{d}x=-\int_{0}^{1}x\mathrm{d}x\int_{x}^{1}\sqrt{1+t^{3}}\mathrm{d}t,
> $$
>
>  交换积分次序并计算得 
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}\int_{0}^{1}x f(x)\mathrm{d}x&=-\int_{0}^{1}\sqrt{1+t^{3}}\mathrm{d}t\int_{0}^{t}x\mathrm{d}x\\&=-\frac{1}{2}\int_{0}^{1}t^{2}\sqrt{1+t^{3}}\mathrm{d}t=-\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{2}{9}(1+t^{3})^{\frac{3}{2}}\bigg|_{0}^{1}\\&=\frac{1}{9}(1-2\sqrt{2}).\end{aligned}
