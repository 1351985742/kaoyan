## 001. 1993 数二

- 题目ID：`1153`
- 题号：`1`
- 题源：1993 数二
- 分类：高等数学 / 一元积分 / 定积分 / 定积分计算 / 三角相关
- 选做：否

求 $\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\frac{x}{1+\cos 2 x}~d x$。

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> $\frac{\pi}{8} - \frac{1}{4}\ln 2$
>
> **解析**
>
> $$
> \begin{align*}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\frac{x}{1+\cos 2 x}~d x &=\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\frac{x}{2\cos^2 x}~d x = \frac{1}{2}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} x~d(\tan x) \\ &= \frac{1}{2} x\tan x \Big|_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} - \frac{1}{2}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\tan x~d x \\ &= \frac{\pi}{8} - \frac{1}{2}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\frac{\sin x}{\cos x}~d x \\ &= \frac{\pi}{8} + \frac{1}{2}\ln|\cos x|\Big|_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \\ &= \frac{\pi}{8} + \frac{1}{2}\ln\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{\pi}{8} - \frac{1}{4}\ln 2.\end{align*}
> $$

## 002. 25版魔法练习册第九章强化 16

- 题目ID：`1154`

- 题源：25版魔法练习册第九章强化 16

- 选做：否

求 $\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\frac{dx}{\sin^2 x+3\cos^2 x}$。

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> $\frac{\pi}{6\sqrt{3}}$
>
> **解析**
>
> $$
> \begin{align*}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\frac{dx}{\sin^2 x+3\cos^2 x} &=\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\frac{dx}{\cos^2 x(3+\tan^2 x)} \\ &\xrightarrow{\text{令 } t=\tan x} \int_0^1\frac{dt}{3+t^2} \\ &= \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\arctan\frac{t}{\sqrt{3}}\Big|_0^1 = \frac{\pi}{6\sqrt{3}}.\end{align*}
> $$

## 003. 25版 660数一二三第 198题

$\int_{\pi}^{\frac{3}{2}\pi}\sin^2\theta\cos^5\theta d\theta = (\quad)$。

$$
\text{(A)} -\frac{8}{105}\qquad\text{(B)} -\frac{4}{35}\qquad\text{(C)} \frac{4}{35}\qquad\text{(D)} \frac{2}{105}
$$

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> A
>
> **解析**
>
> $$
> \begin{align*}\int_{\pi}^{\frac{3}{2}\pi}\sin^2\theta\cos^5\theta d\theta &= \int_{\pi}^{\frac{3}{2}\pi} (\sin^2\theta\cos^4\theta) \cos\theta d\theta \\ &= \int_{\pi}^{\frac{3}{2}\pi} \sin^2\theta (1-\sin^2\theta)^2 d(\sin\theta) \\ &\xrightarrow{\text{令 } u=\sin\theta} \int_{0}^{-1} u^2(1-u^2)^2 du \quad (\text{当 } \theta\in[\pi, \frac{3\pi}{2}] \text{时，} \sin\theta\text{从 }0\text{ 减到 }-1) \\ &= -\int_{-1}^{0} u^2(1-2u^2+u^4) du = \int_{-1}^{0} (-u^2+2u^4-u^6) du \\ &= \left(-\frac{u^3}{3}+\frac{2u^5}{5}-\frac{u^7}{7}\right)\Big|_{-1}^{0} \\ &= -\left[\left(-\frac{(-1)^3}{3}+\frac{2(-1)^5}{5}-\frac{(-1)^7}{7}\right)\right] \\ &= -\left(\frac{1}{3} - \frac{2}{5} + \frac{1}{7}\right) = -\left(\frac{35}{105} - \frac{42}{105} + \frac{15}{105}\right) = -\frac{8}{105}.\end{align*}

## 004. 25版魔法练习册第九章基础 8

- 题目ID：`1156`
- 题号：`4`
- 题源：25版魔法练习册第九章基础 8
- 分类：高等数学 / 一元积分 / 定积分 / 定积分计算 / 三角相关
- 选做：否

$\int_{0}^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\sec^3\theta d\theta =$______。

> [!success]- 答案解析
> **答案**
>
> $\frac{1}{2}[\sqrt{2}+\ln(\sqrt{2}+1)]$
>
> **解析**
>
> $$
> \begin{align*}\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\sec^3\theta d\theta &= \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sec\theta d(\tan\theta) \\ &= \sec\theta\tan\theta\Big|_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} - \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\sec\theta\tan^2\theta d\theta \\ &= \sqrt{2} - \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sec^3\theta d\theta + \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sec\theta d\theta \\ \text{移项得： } 2\int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\sec^3\theta d\theta &= \sqrt{2} + \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}} \sec\theta d\theta \\ \int_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\sec^3\theta d\theta &= \frac{1}{2}\left[\sqrt{2} + \ln|\sec\theta+\tan\theta|\Big|_0^{\frac{\pi}{4}}\right] \\ &= \frac{1}{2}[\sqrt{2} + \ln(\sqrt{2}+1)].\end{align*}
> $$
