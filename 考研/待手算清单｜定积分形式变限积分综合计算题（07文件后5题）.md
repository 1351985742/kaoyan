2. **[008. 25版1000第九章强化24](file:///Users/devilrecal/Downloads/%E6%95%99%E6%9D%90/Obsidian%E9%A2%98%E5%BA%93/01%20%E9%AB%98%E7%AD%89%E6%95%B0%E5%AD%A6/03%20%E4%B8%80%E5%85%83%E7%A7%AF%E5%88%86/03%20%E5%8F%98%E9%99%90%E7%A7%AF%E5%88%86/07%20%E4%BB%A5%E5%AE%9A%E7%A7%AF%E5%88%86%E5%BD%A2%E5%BC%8F%E7%BB%99%E5%87%BA%E7%9A%84%E5%8F%98%E9%99%90%E7%A7%AF%E5%88%86.md#008.%2025%E7%89%881000%E7%AC%AC%E4%B9%9D%E7%AB%A0%E5%BC%BA%E5%8C%9624)（ID: 2124）**

   - 核心任务：拆段求导至二阶导数，或用麦克劳林展开逐项对齐。

3. **[009. 25版1000第九章强化26](file:///Users/devilrecal/Downloads/%E6%95%99%E6%9D%90/Obsidian%E9%A2%98%E5%BA%93/01%20%E9%AB%98%E7%AD%89%E6%95%B0%E5%AD%A6/03%20%E4%B8%80%E5%85%83%E7%A7%AF%E5%88%86/03%20%E5%8F%98%E9%99%90%E7%A7%AF%E5%88%86/07%20%E4%BB%A5%E5%AE%9A%E7%A7%AF%E5%88%86%E5%BD%A2%E5%BC%8F%E7%BB%99%E5%87%BA%E7%9A%84%E5%8F%98%E9%99%90%E7%A7%AF%E5%88%86.md#009.%2025%E7%89%881000%E7%AC%AC%E4%B9%9D%E7%AB%A0%E5%BC%BA%E5%8C%9626)（ID: 2125）**

4. **[004. 24版1000第九章B组13](file:///Users/devilrecal/Downloads/%E6%95%99%E6%9D%90/Obsidian%E9%A2%98%E5%BA%93/01%20%E9%AB%98%E7%AD%89%E6%95%B0%E5%AD%A6/03%20%E4%B8%80%E5%85%83%E7%A7%AF%E5%88%86/03%20%E5%8F%98%E9%99%90%E7%A7%AF%E5%88%86/07%20%E4%BB%A5%E5%AE%9A%E7%A7%AF%E5%88%86%E5%BD%A2%E5%BC%8F%E7%BB%99%E5%87%BA%E7%9A%84%E5%8F%98%E9%99%90%E7%A7%AF%E5%88%86.md#004.%2024%E7%89%881000%E7%AC%AC%E4%B9%9D%E7%AB%A0B%E7%BB%8413)（ID: 2120）**
