11. $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{1 - x^3} = \frac{1}{3}\ln \frac{\sqrt{1 + x + x^2}}{|1 - x|} +\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\arctan \frac{2x + 1}{\sqrt{3}} +C.$

15. $\int \frac{x}{1 - x^3} \, \mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{6} \ln \frac{1 + x + x^2}{(1 - x)^2} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} \arctan \frac{2x + 1}{\sqrt{3}} + C.$
